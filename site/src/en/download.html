First-time setup on Mac

  1. Drag Tacit into Applications

    Open Tacit-{{MAC_VERSION}}.dmg and drag Tacit into the Applications folder.

  2. Allow Tacit to open once

    Open Tacit from Applications. Because the app uses a self-signed certificate without Apple notarization, macOS will block the first launch. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, find Tacit, click Open Anyway, and enter your password.

    If that button does not appear, run this command in Terminal and open Tacit again.

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tacit.app

  3. Complete the setup checklist

    The main window opens and starts the local background service automatically. Follow the checklist at the top of the timeline to grant permissions, pick a summary model, and connect Claude Code or Codex. Closing the window leaves Tacit recording quietly from the menu bar.

Pair with your Mac

Install Tacit-{{ANDROID_VERSION}}.apk and enable on-device recording and transcription. On your Mac, open Settings › Devices and scan the pairing QR code with your phone for end-to-end encrypted sync and full timeline viewing.

Use on its own

You can also use the Android app without a Mac. Recordings stay transcribed on your phone, and you can optionally enable per-conversation AI summaries in settings.

Self-hosted server (Docker)

Linux & Windows · linux/amd64 & linux/arm64 · ghcr.io/jinsooo/tacit-hub

Server deployment guide

The background service runs locally on your Mac by default, so no server is required. To keep an always-on encrypted background service shared across devices, pull the official container image on a Linux server or a Windows PC with Docker Desktop.

docker pull ghcr.io/jinsooo/tacit-hub:{{HUB_VERSION}}
