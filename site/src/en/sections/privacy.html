Your data lives on your devices

If you choose a cloud model for summaries, Tacit sends the relevant text to your configured provider.

Records an AI tool retrieves enter that tool’s session.

Tacit collects no telemetry. The optional relay mailbox handles encrypted sync messages.

Encrypted storage
Tacit encrypts the database, original audio and queued records on your device. macOS Keychain or Android Keystore protects the keys. Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Tacit reads screenshots in memory and discards them.
Capture controls
Screen capture skips common password managers. WeChat capture is off by default and can be enabled on Mac. Pause recording from the menu bar or with a shortcut.
On-device transcription
Tacit transcribes audio on your device by default. It looks for your voice, with an exception for meetings on Mac. Tell people before recording.
Private records
Private records stay out of summaries by default and never appear in automatic session context. AI tools can still retrieve them through a direct query.
Key and token filtering
Tacit blocks known key and token formats before saving. Other formats may pass through.
Delete original records and old summaries
Deleting an original record removes it from the database and immediately hides old summaries that cite it.
