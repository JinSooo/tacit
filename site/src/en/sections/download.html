Download for Mac Download for Android

A Windows app is planned. You can already run the background service on Windows with Docker.

The iOS app is still being explored. You can use Tacit on an Apple silicon Mac or an Android phone.

Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later. Android arm64, Android 9 or later.

Get started on Mac

  1. Drag Tacit into Applications

    Open Tacit-<version>.dmg and drag Tacit into Applications.

  2. Open Tacit for the first time

    Open Tacit from Applications. If macOS blocks it, click Done. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, find Tacit, then click Open Anyway and enter your password.

    If you don’t see that button, run this command in Terminal and open Tacit again.

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tacit.app

  3. Finish setup

    The main window opens and the background service starts. Follow the setup checklist at the top of the timeline. Tacit keeps recording from the menu bar when you close the window.

Get started on Android

Install Tacit-<version>.apk and enable recording and transcription. For end-to-end encrypted sync and timeline viewing, go to Settings › Devices on your Mac and scan the pairing code with your phone. You can also use the app on its own and enable conversation summaries in settings.

Run your own background service

The background service runs on your Mac by default. To share a separate service across devices, run ghcr.io/jinsooo/tacit-hub on Linux or Windows with Docker. See the deployment guide.
